# Payment logo sources

- Orange Money: Wikimedia Commons, `File:Logo_Orange_Money.svg`, original attributed to Orange Côte d’Ivoire.
- Wave: https://www.wave.com/img/nav-logo.png (official website navigation logo).
- MTN MoMo: https://momoapi.mtn.com/content/mtnmomoLogo.svg (official developer portal).
- Moov Money Burkina Faso: logo cropped without recoloring from the official campaign at https://moov-africa.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MoovMoney1040x500.png.

These marks identify payment methods; their availability depends on the country and PayDunya checkout options. They do not imply a partnership.
